While exporting sign-ins for ticket 4471 we pulled the video storage server's
read records for the same week (interne/lectures.csv). They are raw storage
records: the support console itself keeps no log of who watches what.

Each line is one customer video opened from inside Lucarne.
  account   the internal account that opened it
  role      support | engineering | contractor
  camera    where the customer placed the camera
  ticket    the support ticket that justified it, or "-" when there is none

Current rights are in interne/acces-video.yml.
