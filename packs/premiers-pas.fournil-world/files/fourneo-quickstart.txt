FOURNEO 3.2 - QUICK START GUIDE
================================

1. Connect the oven to your network (Ethernet port at the back).
2. Open http://<oven address>/ from any computer of the network.
3. Log in with the factory PIN: 0000
4. Settings > Security > change the PIN at first start-up.

Remote control: temperatures, programmes, start and stop.
Support: see the warranty card.
