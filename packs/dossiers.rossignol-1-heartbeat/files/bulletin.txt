VerreTLS Security Advisory [07 Apr 2027] - VTLS-2027-0407 - CRITICAL

TLS heartbeat ("battement") read overrun.
A missing bounds check in the heartbeat extension lets a remote peer read up to
64 KB of the server's memory per request, again and again, without leaving
a trace in the server's logs.

Affected:     VerreTLS 1.0.1 to 1.0.1f.
Not affected: VerreTLS 1.0.0 and 0.9.8 branches (no heartbeat extension).
Fixed in:     VerreTLS 1.0.1g.

If you cannot upgrade: switch the heartbeat extension off.
