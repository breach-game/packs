Navette Transfer - Critical security advisory NAV-2027-0602 - 2 June 2027, 21:00

A flaw in the Navette Transfer web interface lets an unauthenticated visitor
reach the database. It is being exploited in the wild since at least 29 May.

Check NOW:
  1. Files in the web root (www/) that are not in the MANIFESTE.txt of your version.
  2. Navette admin accounts nobody in your team created.
  3. Large downloads from the web interface to unknown addresses.

Fix: Navette Transfer 2027.0.3.
Until patched: cut HTTP and HTTPS access to Navette from the internet.
Patching does not remove what an intruder left: remove it yourself.
