SILLAGE - how an answer is built (internal note, Fauvette IT)

1. RETRIEVE   The employee asks a question. Sillage looks for relevant content
              in everything that employee can read: files, chats, and the
              mailbox, including emails received from outside the company.
2. FILTER IN  An injection filter drops content that looks like orders given
              to an assistant ("ignore your instructions", "you are now...").
3. ANSWER     The model writes an answer from the question and what is left.
              Everything it retrieved sits in one single context.
4. FILTER OUT Links to outside addresses are removed from the answer.
              Known gap: links written in the "reference" style of Markdown
              are not recognised yet (ticket SIL-311).
5. DISPLAY    The chat window renders the answer. Images are loaded at once,
              without a click, from the allowed domains only:
                - fichiers.fauvette.test   company files
                - apercu.fauvette.test     preview service: fetches the page
                                           at any address it is given, to
                                           show a thumbnail
