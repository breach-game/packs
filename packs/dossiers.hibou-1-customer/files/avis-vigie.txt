VIGIE - security notice to customers - 2028-03-09

On 8 March an unauthorised person had access to our cloud console with the
rights of our support team. With those rights they could, for every customer:
  - watch live video and listen to live audio from any camera
  - read the site address and the name given to each camera
  - read the device configuration, which includes the Wi-Fi network name and
    Wi-Fi key that each camera uses to connect

We have closed that access. We cannot tell you which cameras were watched.
We recommend that you review where your cameras are placed and what they can hear.
