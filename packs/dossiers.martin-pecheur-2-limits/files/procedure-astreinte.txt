BRUME WATER BOARD - ON-CALL PROCEDURE (version of 2022, still in force)

Normal values
  Sodium hydroxide (NaOH), pH adjustment: 100 ppm. Never above 120 ppm in
  normal operation. The pump cannot safely dose more than 150 ppm.
  Chlorine (Cl2): between 0.2 and 2.0 mg/L.

Remote access
  The on-call engineer may connect to the HMI with TelePoste to look at the
  trends. Account: station. Password: see the binder.
